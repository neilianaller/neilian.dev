Story time! Sometime in May, I was in the hospital, one of those keeping watch over my mother-in-law. Around the same time, my mom was also being operated on in Cebu.

Through those incidents, God reminded me of how beautiful marriage always is. Both my papa and my father-in-law stayed by their spouses' side in the hospital, knowing everything would be alright in time.

## A promise I remember

Then I remembered the reception at our wedding, Cacay's and mine. I promised to serve her always, and she promised to love me always and in all ways.

---

## That's it

The very reason I built the Marriage is Beautiful web app is that I was inspired to share how beautiful marriage is.

With so many people afraid of commitment nowadays, I hope this platform helps them realize that marriage is not scary. **It's beautiful.**
