Story time! Fr. Elric is currently the parish priest of Nuestra Señora de Guadalupe in Lantapan, Bukidnon. My first interaction with Padre was at a Parish Pastoral Council (PPC) meeting, where we talked about his passion project: his daily reflection vlogs called **"Wanminit with Fr. Elric."**

## What amazed me

I was amazed by Padre's passion, his commitment to post daily, and that he doesn't post just any video. These are reflections made with intention. They aren't random videos; they are carefully prepared. I was amazed because I once posted daily short reflections myself, and I know how hard it is to stay consistent.

## A challenge from a saint

Then the late Pope Francis canonized Blessed Carlo Acutis. And I discovered that St. Carlo built a digital catalog of Eucharistic Miracles ([miracolieucaristici.org](https://www.miracolieucaristici.org/)). How great is that! The idea is so simple, but so impactful.

So I challenged myself. Not to become a saint (hopefully, though), but to build something with impact. (Well, not the same impact as St. Carlo 🤣.)

## The homilies that stayed with me

Then I remembered visiting the shrine of Venerable Teofilo Camomot (a Servant of God at the time) in Carcar, Cebu. The compilation of his homilies stuck with me.

> "Wow, someone actually collected and kept his homilies."

So I decided to build a compilation of homilies. But the question was: whose homilies? 🤣 Then I remembered Fr. Elric and his daily one-minute reflection vlogs.

> "Why don't I build a repository of his vlogs, so that when Padre becomes a saint, anyone can access his daily reflections?"

So I built it, approached Padre, and offered it to him as a gift for his passion.

---

## That's it

The very reason I built Wanminit with Fr. Elric Web is that I want the faithful to easily access his inspiring reflection videos. That way, when the time comes, a repository of his good works through social media will stay forever.
