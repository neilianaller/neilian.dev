Fr. Elric has shared daily one-minute homilies for years across various social feeds. However, social algorithms bury old teachings and distract listeners with autoplay ads. 🕊️

### An Algorithmic-Free Archive

This web application serves as a permanent, searchable sanctuary for every homily:

- **Calendar-Based Scripture Lookup** — Quickly find reflections by liturgical date or biblical passage. 📖
- **Audio First** — High-fidelity audio playback that remembers your listening place.
- **Fast & Minimal** — Loads in under 400 milliseconds on mobile devices.

Preserving spiritual words in a dedicated medium gives listeners space to absorb meaning at their own pace.
